Controls:
W/S/A/D to Move

Hints for Reconstruction

  • Three.js uses a tree hierarchy. Link objects using parent.add(child);
  • Since the WASD keys move the head, the head acts as the "root" parent.
  • Once attached, a child's position becomes relative to its parent's center, not the world.
  • You will need to manually reset the local coordinates (e.g., torso.position.set(0, -1.15, 0);) after linking so they snap to the correct anatomical spot.
  • Don't forget to reset the rotation of your components to 0 so they face forward!
